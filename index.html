Cartographer

A daily world geography quiz for iPhone.

Everything stays on your phone

No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads. Cartographer makes no network requests at all. See the privacy policy.

Frequently asked

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Everyone gets the same ten questions each day, and you get one attempt. The puzzle is generated from the date itself, so it needs no server and works offline. Your result comes with a spoiler-free grid you can share anywhere.

What's the difference between the three levels?

Explorer asks about well-known places, untimed, with four options. Navigator widens the net, adds a fifth option and a 20-second clock. Cartographer draws from the most obscure entries, offers six options and gives you 12 seconds — and the wrong answers get closer to the right one.

Can I stop the app recording my results?

Yes. Settings → Record quiz history turns it off. Existing results are kept until you choose Delete All History, which erases them permanently.

I lost my streak / my history disappeared

History lives only on the device, so it doesn't transfer between phones and isn't in iCloud. Deleting the app deletes it. There's no way to recover it — sorry.

I found a wrong answer

Please tell us, with the exact question. Every fact was checked against authoritative sources, but a few regions have genuinely contested or shared capitals, and errors are possible. Corrections are welcome and get fixed quickly.
