Privacy Policy

Cartographer · Last updated 4 October 2026

The short version. Cartographer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The iPhone app does not connect to the internet, and the website sends nothing back once it has loaded.

What the app stores

Cartographer saves the following only on your device:

  • Your quiz history: date, quiz name, difficulty, score, and duration
  • Your daily streak and Speed Run best times
  • Your settings, such as the difficulty you last chose

This is stored locally using Apple's on-device database. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, and never shared with a third party. We operate no servers and have no way to see it.

On the website

The Cartographer website stores the same kinds of information, in your browser's local storage, on your device. It is never uploaded. The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, analytics or ads, and uses no cookies.

Like any website, the pages are delivered by hosting providers (currently Cloudflare Pages, and GitHub Pages for this policy and the support page), whose servers may record standard technical details such as your IP address when you load them. We don't receive or use that information. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

Your control over it

  • Recording is on by default and can be switched off at any time in Settings → Record quiz history.
  • Turning it off stops new results being saved and leaves existing ones intact.
  • Settings → Delete All History permanently removes everything stored.
  • Deleting the app removes all of it from your device. On the website, clearing this site's data in your browser does the same.

Sharing

When you choose to share a result or a streak, the app hands an image or a short line of text to the iOS share sheet. What happens after that is between you and the app you send it to. Cartographer transmits nothing on its own.

Children

Cartographer is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Third parties

Cartographer includes no third-party SDKs, no analytics libraries, and no advertising networks.
